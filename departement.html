Medical Teams

Two teams that keep every corner of the city covered.

🚑 External Medical Team

The field team. They handle every emergency call outside, all across the city.

  • Respond to emergency calls within 5 minutes
  • Provide on-scene care and patient treatment
  • Coordinate with Despatch 911 for full city coverage
  • Respect safe zones and avoid red zones

🏥 Internal Medical Team

The hospital team. They take care of everything inside the medical center.

  • Receive and stabilize patients brought in by the field team
  • Manage internal care, departments and medication
  • Prepare medical records and patient documentation
  • Keep the medical center organized and stocked
