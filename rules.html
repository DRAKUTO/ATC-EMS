WELCOME TO ATLANTIC ROLEPLAY EMS

Emergency Responders of Atlantic Roleplay

As part of Atlantic Roleplay Emergency Medical Services, you represent the highest standard of medical care in our city. Our team is dedicated to helping others, providing emergency medical response, and maintaining professional medical roleplay standards.

We operate as a cohesive unit where every medic plays a vital role in our community's safety and well-being. Excellence, professionalism, and teamwork are not just expectations - they are our foundation.

1. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

✅ DO's

  • Always maintain professional behavior
  • Use proper medical terminology
  • Respect all colleagues, citizens, and staff
  • Follow instructions from EMS leadership and server staff

❌ DON'T

  • No off-duty medical treatment without permission
  • No negative behavior toward team members or other players

2. ACTION GUIDELINES

✅ DO's

  • Attend to emergency situations within 5 minutes when on-duty
  • Follow team structure: Medical Staff → Team Leader → Head of Medical Team → Assistant Director → Director
  • Complete Patient Care documentation after every situation

❌ DON'T

  • NO hurrying to take all calls - distribute work equally
  • ABSOLUTELY NO providing care in unauthorized locations
  • NO medical help in dangerous locations

3. UNIFORM & EQUIPMENT

✅ DO's

  • Wear assigned ATLANTIC ROLEPLAY EMS uniform while on-duty
  • Keep medical equipment organized - CANNOT be traded or given to others
  • Return vehicles to stations after EVERY use
  • If accident occurs, contact dispatch immediately

❌ DON'T

  • AIRCRAFT USE NOT ALLOWED until further notice
  • No personal vehicles during emergency calls

4. COMMUNICATION

✅ DO's

  • Must be active on SHORT RADIO for coordination
  • Use Discord for team coordination and announcements
  • Clear and professional communication at all times
  • Report vehicle issues immediately to dispatch
  • Notify dispatch when starting/ending shifts

❌ DON'T

  • No off-topic discussions on emergency channels
  • No personal conversations on work frequencies

TEAM WORK & COLLEAGUE RESPECT

⚖️ Fair Call Distribution

  • Wait your turn for emergency calls
  • Don't take others' calls - respect dispatch order
  • Assist fellow medics when they need support
  • Share important cases among available units

🏗️ Respect Structure

  • Follow instructions from senior medics
  • Respect decisions of supervisors and medical team
  • Support new medics during training
  • Report concerns through proper channels

🚫 Not Allowed

  • Disputes over calls or patients
  • Undermining colleagues' position
  • Taking all emergency responses
  • Disrespecting leadership decisions

"We succeed as a team"

STAFF PROTOCOL

🛡️ Server Staff

  • Always follow staff instructions immediately
  • Respect staff decisions without disagreement
  • Report concerns to EMS command first, then staff if needed
  • Understand staff have final say in all matters

📋 Issue Resolution

  1. Discuss with EMS supervisor first
  2. Escalate to Chief Medic if unresolved
  3. Involve staff only when medically necessary
  4. Accept final decisions professionally

Staff respect ensures smooth operations

RESTRICTED MEDICAL ZONES

🚫 PROHIBITED AREAS FOR MEDICAL SERVICES

  • ALL Unsafe Areas
  • Unauthorized Facilities
  • High Risk Locations
  • Any Area with Ongoing Issues
  • Security Operation Locations

🦺 SAFETY PROTOCOL

"When unsafe situations are detected, EMS must:"

  1. IMMEDIATELY move to safe distance
  2. Notify dispatch of unsafe conditions
  3. Wait for security clearance before entering
  4. Never risk personal safety

⚠️ CONSEQUENCES

Violating safety zones will result in:

  • Immediate suspension from duty
  • Mandatory safety retraining
  • Possible removal for repeated issues

Remember: Safety first for all team members
